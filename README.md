## Main statement

The Riemann Hypothesis states that every non-trivial zero of the Riemann zeta function ζ(s) has real part 1/2.

## Prize information

The Clay Mathematics Institute does not accept direct submissions of proposed solutions to the Millennium Prize Problems. Its published rules require publication in a qualifying outlet, at least two years after publication, and general acceptance by the global mathematics community before a proposed solution can be considered for the prize. See the [official rules](https://www.claymath.org/millennium-problems/rules/) and the [official Riemann Hypothesis page](https://www.claymath.org/millennium/riemann-hypothesis/).
